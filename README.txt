Piesquare Technologies - Website Prototype
====================================

Files
  index.html      Home page and Careers page (IT Training and Job Assistance)
  img/            Logo files (icon mark and header wordmark)
  css/style.css   All styling (colors and fonts are variables at the top)
  js/main.js      Product preview tabs, page switching, form messages

How to open
  Double-click index.html. No installation needed. Internet is needed
  only to load the Google Fonts.

How to put it online
  Upload the whole folder to any hosting (cPanel, Netlify, GitHub Pages).

Things to replace
  - Text, course names, durations and dashboard sample data
  - Add your phone, email, address and social links
  - Contact and training forms only show a thank-you message.
    Connect them to email, WhatsApp or a backend to receive enquiries.
